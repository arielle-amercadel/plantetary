# Plantetary Privacy Notice

**Effective date: [DATE — to be set when published]**

Plantetary is a cozy habit and quest tracker. This notice explains what information Plantetary handles, where it's kept, and how to contact us about it. In this notice, "Plantetary," "we" and "us" refer to Plantetary.

## The short version

- **You can play without an account.** As a guest, your garden stays in your browser on your device.
- **If you sign in, we store your email address and your garden** with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, so your progress can follow you between devices.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** Plantetary doesn't load advertising or analytics tools, and doesn't sell your information.
- **You can ask us to delete your account** by emailing **hello.plantetary@gmail.com**.

## What Plantetary collects

**If you sign in:**

- **Your email address,** used to send you sign-in links and to identify your account.
- **Sign-in records kept by Supabase's sign-in service,** such as when your account was created and when you last signed in. Supabase's service may also record technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, as part of keeping sign-ins secure.

**Your garden** (stored in your browser, and also in your account if you use a cloud garden):

- the quests you create, including their titles, notes, categories, difficulty, schedules and due dates;
- your completion history (which quests you completed, and on which days), XP, level and streaks;
- the plants you've discovered or been gifted.

Quest titles and notes are free text you write yourself. Please avoid putting sensitive personal information in them.

**Profile and reminder settings:** a display name, an avatar chosen from Plantetary's set (you can't upload photos), and reminder preferences. These are saved **only in your browser**, even if you're signed in. Plantetary doesn't currently send reminders or notifications.

Plantetary doesn't ask for your name, phone number, address, payment details or location.

## Guests and signed-in players

- **As a guest,** everything stays in your browser on that device. None of it is sent to Plantetary's database.
- **When you sign in,** you choose what happens. You can start a fresh cloud garden, keep playing with the garden in this browser (which stays on this device), or import your browser garden into your account. Your browser garden is copied to your account only if you choose to import it.
- **Signing in or out** doesn't delete or change the garden saved in your browser.

## Browser storage and cookies

Plantetary uses your browser's storage to work:

- **Local storage:** your browser garden and its safety copies, your profile and your reminder settings. These stay until you reset them in **Settings** or clear your browser's site data.
- **Session storage** (cleared when you close the tab):
  - your choice to keep playing on this device;
  - if a cloud save couldn't finish, a temporary copy of those unsaved changes, so you can download them. It's labelled with a partly hidden version of your email address, for example `mi•••@example.com`.
- **One cookie, for sign-in.** When you sign in, Supabase's sign-in tools store your session in a cookie so you stay signed in. It contains your session details, including your account's email address and ID. It lasts up to 400 days unless you sign out or clear it; signing out removes it from that browser.

Plantetary doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies, and doesn't track you across other websites. Because there's no cross-site tracking, Plantetary doesn't respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

## Cloud saves, backups and how long they're kept

If you use a cloud garden, Supabase stores your garden for your account. Plantetary also keeps **backup copies** of your cloud garden in your account. These help protect your progress against data loss, for example if two devices save at the same time, or if something goes wrong while importing or updating a save:

- **Daily copies:** when your garden changes, the previous version is copied, at most once a day. The newest 14 are kept.
- **Conflict copies:** if two devices save at once, the version that couldn't be saved is kept. The newest 20 are kept.
- **Import copies:** if you import your browser garden, your previous cloud garden is copied first. The newest 5 are kept.
- **Upgrade copies:** if a newer version of Plantetary updates an older save's format, the original is kept. The newest 5 are kept.

Backups are kept **by number, not by date**. A backup is removed only when newer copies of the same kind replace it, or when your account is deleted. If you rarely play, older copies may be kept for a long time. Backups aren't shown in the app.

**Copies made for maintenance:** occasionally, Plantetary may download a copy of its database, for example before an update. These copies are kept privately. Our practice is to delete them by hand within 30 days.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** stores signed-in players' accounts, gardens and backups, and runs sign-in, including sending sign-in emails.
- **Vercel** hosts the Plantetary website. Like most hosting providers, it may log technical information about visits, such as IP addresses and browser type.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies and retention practices, which Plantetary doesn't control. If Plantetary starts using another service that handles your information, for example a separate email provider for sign-in emails, this notice will be updated.

## How your information is protected

- Plantetary is only served over secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Database rules let each signed-in account read and change only its own garden and backups. Visitors who aren't signed in can't read the database at all.
- Plantetary doesn't load scripts from advertising or analytics companies.

No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

## Your choices: access and deletion

**See or copy your information:** **Settings → Data management → Export data as JSON** downloads the garden you're currently playing, with your profile and reminder settings. It doesn't include your email address or cloud backups. You can also email us with questions about the information connected to your account.

**Delete the data in your browser:** while you're playing the garden saved in your browser, **Settings → Reset Plantetary data** removes it from this device, along with its safety copies and your profile and reminder settings. You can also clear the site's data in your browser settings at any time.

**Delete your account:**

1. Email **hello.plantetary@gmail.com** from the email address of the account you want deleted, and say you'd like it deleted.
2. We'll check that the request comes from the account's owner. We may reply to that address to confirm.
3. We then delete the account by hand. Deletion isn't automatic.

## What happens after your account is deleted

When we delete your account, its stored information is removed from Plantetary's database: your sign-in account, your cloud garden and all of its backups.

Some copies are outside Plantetary's database, or outside our control:

- **Your devices:** your browser garden and settings stay on your devices until you reset them or clear your browser's data. The sign-in cookie may stay in your browser until you sign out or clear it, but it no longer leads to an account.
- **Files you downloaded,** such as exports or recovery files, stay wherever you saved them.
- **Maintenance copies** made before the deletion may still contain your information until they're deleted by hand within 30 days, as described above.
- **Supabase and Vercel** may keep backups or logs for a time under their own policies.

## Children's privacy

Plantetary is a general productivity app and isn't specifically directed to children under 13. Plantetary doesn't ask for your age and doesn't verify it. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at **hello.plantetary@gmail.com**, and you can ask us to delete it as described above.
