# Plantetary Privacy Notice

**Effective date: [DATE — to be set when published]**

Plantetary is a cozy habit and quest tracker. This notice is from **Plantetary — an independent personal project**. It explains what information Plantetary handles, where it's kept, and how to contact us about it. In this notice, "Plantetary," "we" and "us" refer to Plantetary.

## The short version

- **You can play without an account.** As a guest, your garden stays in your browser on your device.
- **If you sign in, we store your email address** with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, **and your garden too if you use a cloud garden**, so your progress can follow you between devices.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** Plantetary doesn't load advertising or analytics tools, and doesn't sell your information.
- **Accounts are for people 13 and older.**
- **You can ask to see, correct or delete your information** by emailing **hello.plantetary@gmail.com**.

## What Plantetary collects

**If you ask for a sign-in link or sign in:**

- **Your email address,** used to send you sign-in links and to identify your account. Asking for a sign-in link creates an account for that address with Supabase, even if the link is never used. You're only signed in once you open the link and finish signing in.
- **Sign-in records kept by Supabase's sign-in service,** such as when your account was created and when you last signed in. Supabase's service may also record technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, as part of keeping sign-ins secure.

**Your garden** (stored in your browser, and also in your account if you use a cloud garden):

- the quests you create, including their titles, notes, categories, difficulty, schedules and due dates;
- your completion history (which quests you completed, and on which days), XP, level and streaks;
- the plants you've discovered or been gifted.

Plantetary is a general habit and quest tracker. Quest titles and notes are free text you write yourself, so please avoid putting sensitive personal information in them, such as details about your health, finances or other private matters.

**Profile and reminder settings:** a display name, an avatar chosen from Plantetary's set (you can't upload photos), and reminder preferences. These are saved **only in your browser**, even if you're signed in. Plantetary doesn't currently send reminders or notifications.

Plantetary doesn't require your real name, birthday, phone number, address, payment details or location. You can optionally choose a display name.

## Guests and signed-in players

- **As a guest,** everything stays in your browser on that device. None of it is sent to Plantetary's database, and Plantetary can't see it or delete it for you. To remove it, use **Settings → Reset Plantetary data**, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
- **When you sign in,** your email address is stored with Supabase. You then choose what happens to your garden: start a fresh cloud garden, keep playing with the garden in this browser (which stays on this device), or import your browser garden into your account. Your garden is stored with Supabase only if you use a cloud garden, and your browser garden is copied to your account only if you choose to import it.
- **Signing in or out** doesn't delete or change the garden saved in your browser, and signing out doesn't delete your cloud garden.

## Browser storage and cookies

Plantetary uses your browser's storage to work:

- **Local storage:** your browser garden and its safety copies, your profile and your reminder settings. These stay until you reset them in **Settings** or clear your browser's site data.
- **Session storage** (cleared when you close the tab):
  - your choice to keep playing on this device;
  - if some cloud changes hadn't been saved yet (for example, a save failed or the page closed before it finished), a temporary copy of them, so you can download them. It's labelled with a partly hidden version of your email address, for example `mi•••@example.com`.
- **Cookies, only for sign-in.** Supabase's sign-in tools use cookies:
  - **Your session:** when you sign in, your session is stored in a cookie so you stay signed in. A long session may be split across a few cookies. It contains your session details, including your account's email address and ID.
  - **Temporary sign-in codes:** when you ask for a sign-in link, codes used to finish signing in are also stored in cookies.
  - **How long they last:** some of these cookies can last up to 400 days, depending on their purpose. Signing out ends your signed-in session in that browser, but may not remove every temporary sign-in cookie. Clearing this site's cookies in your browser removes them all.

Plantetary doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies, and doesn't track you across other websites. Because there's no cross-site tracking, Plantetary doesn't respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

## Cloud saves, backups and how long they're kept

If you use a cloud garden, Supabase stores your garden for your account. Plantetary also keeps **backup copies** of your cloud garden in your account. These help protect your progress against data loss, for example if two devices save at the same time, or if something goes wrong while importing or updating a save:

- **Daily copies:** when your garden changes, the previous version is copied, at most once a day. The newest 14 are kept.
- **Conflict copies:** if two devices save at once, the version that couldn't be saved is kept. The newest 20 are kept.
- **Import copies:** if you import your browser garden, your previous cloud garden is copied first. The newest 5 are kept.
- **Upgrade copies:** if a newer version of Plantetary updates an older save's format, the original is kept. The newest 5 are kept.

Backups are currently kept **by number, not by date**. A backup is removed only when newer copies of the same kind replace it, or when your account is deleted. If you rarely play, older copies may be kept indefinitely. Backups aren't shown in the app, and players can't change or delete them.

**Copies made for maintenance:** occasionally, Plantetary may download a private copy of its database, for example before an update. Our practice is to delete these copies by hand within 30 days.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** stores signed-in players' accounts, gardens and backups, and runs sign-in, including sending sign-in emails. See [Supabase's privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).
- **Vercel** hosts the Plantetary website. Like most hosting providers, it may process technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, as part of providing its service. See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies and retention practices, which Plantetary doesn't control. If Plantetary starts using another service that handles your information, for example a separate email provider for sign-in emails, this notice will be updated.

## How your information is protected

- Plantetary is only served over secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Sign-in is handled by Supabase's authentication service, using emailed sign-in links rather than passwords.
- Database rules let each signed-in account read only its own garden and backups, and change only its own garden. Backups are added automatically and can't be changed or deleted by players. Visitors who aren't signed in can't read the database at all.
- Plantetary doesn't load scripts from advertising or analytics companies.

No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

## Your choices: access, correction and deletion

**Ask to see, correct or delete your information:** email **hello.plantetary@gmail.com** from your account's email address and say what you'd like: a copy of the information connected to your account, a correction of something that's inaccurate, or deletion of your account and its cloud data. Requests are handled by hand. We'll check that the request comes from the account's owner first, and we may reply to that address to confirm. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, or sooner when the law requires it.

**Correct things yourself:** you can change your quests, profile and settings at any time in the app.

**Take a copy yourself:** **Settings → Data management → Export data as JSON** downloads the garden you're currently playing, with your profile and reminder settings. It doesn't include your email address or other sign-in information, or your cloud backups.

**Delete the data in your browser:** while you're playing the garden saved in your browser, **Settings → Reset Plantetary data** removes it from this device, along with its safety copies and your profile and reminder settings. You can also clear the site's data in your browser settings at any time.

**Delete your account:**

1. Email **hello.plantetary@gmail.com** from the email address of the account you want deleted, and say you'd like it deleted.
2. We'll check that the request comes from the account's owner. We may reply to that address to confirm.
3. We then delete the account by hand. Deletion isn't automatic or immediate: we aim to complete it within 30 days of verifying the request, or sooner when the law requires it.

## What happens after your account is deleted

When we delete your account, the information linked to it in Plantetary's database is removed: your sign-in account, your cloud garden and all of its backups.

Some information may remain for a time outside Plantetary's database, or outside our control:

- **Your devices:** your browser garden and settings stay on your devices until you reset them or clear your browser's data. Sign-in cookies may stay in your browser until you sign out or clear them, but they no longer lead to an account.
- **Files you downloaded,** such as exports or recovery files, stay wherever you saved them.
- **Maintenance copies** made before the deletion may still contain your information until they're deleted by hand within 30 days, as described above.
- **Supabase and Vercel** may keep information for a time in their own backups, security logs or other systems, under their own retention practices and legal requirements.

## Children's privacy

Plantetary accounts are for people 13 and older; teenagers aged 13 to 17 are welcome. Plantetary is a general habit and productivity tracker and isn't directed to children under 13. Before you can ask for a sign-in link, you confirm that you're at least 13 years old. Plantetary doesn't collect birthdays and doesn't independently verify ages. If we learn that an account may belong to a child under 13, we'll promptly look into it and take appropriate steps to delete it. If you have a concern, contact us at **hello.plantetary@gmail.com**.
